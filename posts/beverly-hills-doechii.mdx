## Steps

1. Crab Walk x8 - starting on left side
2. Step Back + Charleston Variation x4 - starting on left side, guide movement with your upper arm and bounce back like you are barging against a door
3. Chest Isolation x8 counts - isolate your chest making a full rotation, on the 8-and count, push your chest in and lead with that motion
4. Running Man x8 - starting on right foot
5. Heel Toe Travel x3 - on the last one, flick and land back on your left foot, you can make this easier by leaning back and to the right so that when you flick you don't lose your balance
6. Spin x2 counts - start this by unraveling your right foot over your left foot and kick back twice throughout the spin to give yourself momentum
7. Running Man x3 - on the last step, clap your hands together to close the choreo on the 1
