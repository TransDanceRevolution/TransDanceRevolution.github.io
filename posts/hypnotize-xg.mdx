## Steps

1. Running Man x4 - starting on right foot
2. Switchblade/Heel Toe x1 (x2 counts) - starting on right foot
3. Heel Toe + Toe Tap Combo x2 counts - starting on right foot
4. Spin x2 counts - pivoting on left foot, kicking out on right foot
5. T-Step x4 counts - to right
6. T-Step x4 counts - to left
7. Happy Feet Combo x4 counts - starting on left side
8. Criss-Cross x2 counts
9. W-Step x2 counts
