## Steps

1. Step out on heels and then back x2 counts - starting on right foot
2. Happy Feet combo x4
3. Criss-Cross x2
4. Running Man x4 - starting on right foot
5. Running Man Stepback x2 - starting on right foot
6. Miami Step x4 - starting on left foot
7. Robot Shuffle x2 - starting on right foot
